Anthropic · Claude

The matching Claude decision ladder

Claude Sonnet 5.5everyday · $2 / $10
Best routine balance for coding, data analysis, content creation, visual work, and agentic tool use when you care about speed and cost.
Claude Haiku 4.5background · $1 / $5
High-volume research workers, classification, summarization, subagents, prototypes, and straightforward tasks with tight cost or latency limits.
Claude Opus 5.5 · Mediumhard/agentic · $4 / $20
Long-running coding agents, large refactors, complex systems engineering, computer use, and knowledge work that must remain coherent for hours.
Claude Fable 5.1 · Highmaximum · $10 / $50
Use when Opus at high effort still falls short: multistep deep research, demanding reasoning, and long-horizon work carried through to a finished deliverable.

Safe homelab rule for either provider: Luna or Haiku may inspect, summarize, and propose. GPT-6.1 Sol or Sonnet may implement reversible, reviewed changes. Astra, Opus, or Fable should plan and verify changes touching networking, storage, credentials, backups, public exposure, or several dependent services. Keep a human approval step for destructive or externally visible actions.
